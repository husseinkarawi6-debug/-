Flat 2D shadow-theatre cut-out animation in the tradition of 1920s silhouette films and Arabic khayal al-zill, filmed with a 1930s multiplane camera. Figures are opaque black card with filigree scissor-cut edges; every garment is one flat panel of translucent dyed hide glowing like stained glass, with no skin showing through. Backlit parchment layers, deep parallax, hard backlight, long flat cast shadows, a silent-film colour tint per scene, fine film grain, soft vignette, figures animated on twos. The only saturated colour is deep burgundy on his clothing. Flat 2D, not 3D, not CGI, not photoreal. Vertical 9:16. Keep the lower 40% of every frame calm and dark.

THE MAN, identical in every shot: broad shoulders, a big crown of thick dark wavy-curly hair cut as fine paper tendrils, a short beard cut as a jagged edge along the jaw, thin wire glasses with only the temple arm glinting behind his ear. His head and face are solid black with no eyes and no features; he is seen only in side profile against light, from behind, or as hands and cast shadows. He never turns toward the camera and never speaks. His clothes change completely at every new place, exactly as written. Other people appear only as faceless cut-out hands or plain shadows; the recurring one is a plump hand heavy with rings and a carnelian signet ring.

Shot 1 (0-2s): Steel-blue stone prison cell. In a ragged grey-brown sackcloth tunic with iron cuffs, he sits on a stone bench in profile while the giant shadow of iron pincers on the wall closes and jerks back with a small black molar. The iron door slams; bar shadows sweep the frame.
Shot 2 (2-5s): Bare chamber at dusk. In a white linen night-thobe he kneels in profile; a small bird of pale light is drawn out of his chest on a glowing thread and flies out of the window. On a rooftop at sunset he hauls on a rope tied to a huge sinking paper sun; it slides under anyway and blue darkness falls.
Shot 3 (5-7s): Night blizzard over an endless white plain. In a bulky ochre fleece-edged sheepskin coat and wool head-wrap, small on the horizon, he leads one balking camel by its halter, a shivering monkey on the saddle.
Shot 4 (7-10s): Umber tannery under black clouds. In a leather tanner's apron with one burgundy strap he heaves a dripping hide onto a wooden frame; a black cloud closes over the last sliver of sun and the light dies.
Shot 5 (10-14s): Desert gorge at night, camera behind him at a tiny campfire; in an ochre dishdasha and fur vest he lowers a skewered lizard, shoulders heaving. A colossal bear rears behind the fire against the huge moon. At dawn, in a straw farmer's tunic, he casts grain in one wide arc onto cracked earth; the cracks are lips stitched shut and press closed.
Shot 6 (14-19s): Kneeling at a red-amber clay brazier he blows on the embers and smoke rolls back over him. In a dark aba drawn over his head like a hood he walks bent through a dusk alley as shadow hands point at him. At his carved door, in a faded merchant's bisht with frayed burgundy trim, he lays the iron house key in the ringed hand, which snaps a copper coin into four and drops one quarter into his palm.
Shot 7 (19-23s): On sunset market steps, in a patched traveller's cloak, he trades his last boot for a loaf; on a long wall his shadow walks beside a companion's shadow, which crumbles into blowing sand. Sea-green harbour at night: in a porter's vest and burgundy head-rag he bends under a coil of rope while a giant hawser rears like a sea serpent and slams a billowing sail again and again; at 23s it snaps taut and the frame flashes white.
Shot 8 (23-26s): From the white, a golden-dawn courtyard and a towering dead-black brass-studded door. In a clean white thobe and his one old black bisht with a glowing burgundy lining he stands small, back to camera; the door's shadow swallows his and his head sinks.
Shot 9 (26-29s): His hand trembles a finger's width from the brass knocker and never touches it; a hatch snaps open and the ringed hand dangles one coin on a string. His hand lowers; he walks away toward the rising sun, back to camera, rising upright, his long shadow thrown back across the door. The coin drops into the dust; the hatch slams.
Shot 10 (29-30s): A present-day room of black cut-outs, trailing pothos vines and a tall glowing window. From behind, in a burgundy fine-knit turtleneck and black pleated trousers, he raises his bowed head until upright.

Exactly one man in the flesh, always fully clothed. No blood, no injury; hardship only through shadows and objects. No on-screen text, subtitles, letters, numbers, logos or watermark. Solemn, tragic, dignified, never comic.
